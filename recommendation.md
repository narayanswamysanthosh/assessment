## Recommended Approach

Start with a limited pilot in which care managers use the Logistic Regression ranking model to prioritize outreach for approximately the highest-risk 5% of discharges each week.
The model demonstrated the strongest overall validation performance while maintaining the best probability calibration among evaluated candidates.
Although 63.6% of patients identified within the top 5% risk segment were subsequently readmitted in validation data, the dataset is synthetic and does not establish clinical effectiveness.
The recommendation is therefore to use the model strictly as a prioritization aid rather than an automated decision system.
Expansion beyond the pilot should depend on prospective validation using real hospital data, measurement of outreach impact, and ongoing monitoring for calibration drift, subgroup performance differences, and operational workflow fit.
